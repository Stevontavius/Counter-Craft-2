# WHAT THE INSTALLER CHANGES ON YOUR COMPUTER
- It reads your Counter-Strike installs and never changes them.
- It builds the asset pack in the folder you choose. The pack stays there, so a later update can skip building when nothing changed.
- It adds a new Counter-Craft 2 profile with its own game folder (Minecraft Launcher), or a new Counter-Craft 2 instance (Prism Launcher or MultiMC), or updates one it made before. Your other profiles, instances and worlds aren't changed.
- Before it changes the Minecraft Launcher's profiles file, it keeps a copy next to it (launcher_profiles.json.cc2-backup).
- It doesn't save your choices. The next time it starts, it looks for your games again.
- It keeps a log of its last run in your temporary folder (cc2-installer.log).
